Understanding your risk colour

The warning bands use the original, unrounded relative humidity reading. Humidity is rounded only for display.

Low risk60% RH or below
Moderate riskAbove 60%, up to 70% RH
High riskAbove 70% RH

40–60% RH is the preferred indoor range used in the project research. Very dry air below 40% is outside that range, even though this mould-warning scale stays green.

These are project warning bands, not an HHSRS assessment. Duration, cold surfaces and other conditions also matter. The sustained-risk rule still needs team agreement.
